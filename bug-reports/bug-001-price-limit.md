Bug report #001 - Price limit validation

Summary
A product category intended to display items priced at €50 or less displays a product priced above the specified limit.

Environment
- Platform: Mobile application
- Device: Android 16
- Feature: Product category / price limit

Preconditions
The user has access to the product category with the €50 price limit.

Steps to reproduce
1. Open the mobile app shopping application
2. Navigate to the category intended to display products priced up to €50
3. Browse the products displayed in the category
4. Check the price of the products shown

Expected result
All products displayed in the category should have a price of €50 or less

Actual result
A product priced at €57.99 is displayed in the category

Reproducibility
Reproduced multiple times

Severity
Medium

Notes
This is a personal QA portfolio excercise. Confidencial information, custumer information, test cycle information,
and testing materials have been omitted.
